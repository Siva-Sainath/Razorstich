# RazorStitch: 60-Second Pitch (Hive NOVELTY Track)

**RazorStitch** transforms payment failure recovery from a blind, rule-based guessing game into an intelligent, data-driven science. By leveraging a Deep Q-Network (DQN) trained on the complex anatomy of transaction failures, our agent learns the optimal recovery strategy—balancing maximum revenue retrieval against the preservation of customer trust. Instead of spamming users with immediate retries or generic payment links, RazorStitch acts as a razor-sharp recovery policy with a built-in "Trust Budget" and a comprehensive audit trail, ensuring every intervention is calculated, compliant, and highly effective.

**Why RazorStitch is a Game Changer:**
- **Dynamic Decision Making:** Replaces static `failure_rules` with a reinforcement learning agent that adapts to error codes, banks, and contextual variables.
- **Optimized Net Value:** Outperforms standard failure rules, generating an incremental 36,187 INR net value per seed in our simulated benchmarks.
- **Trust Budget Preservation:** Drastically reduces customer friction, cutting duplicate and aggressive retry incidents by nearly 50% compared to brute-force baselines (`always_payment_link`).
- **Full Auditability:** Every action, from Q-value calculation to webhook success, is logged in a transparent audit trail, crucial for financial compliance.
- **Safe Exploration:** Uses simulated environments to safely train the policy without risking live customer relationships, seamlessly deploying the optimized agent to production.
